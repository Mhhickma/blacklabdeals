Why I started this site

Shopping online can get noisy fast. Black Lab Deals was created to give shoppers a cleaner way to browse current Amazon product information and product picks by category.

The goal is simple: make the site easy to scan, easy to understand, and clear about the fact that final product details should always be confirmed on Amazon before purchase.

What Black Lab Deals helps with

  • Find a category fast Start with the type of product you are shopping for and jump straight into a focused list of current product picks.
  • Browse without the noise The site keeps the wording simple and focuses on helpful product details instead of flashy claims.
  • Double-check before buying Each product card gives you a direct path to Amazon so you can confirm the final price, shipping, coupon status, and availability before you purchase.

Built and maintained by Matt

Black Lab Deals is not meant to feel like a faceless shopping page. It is a real site run by a real person who wants the browsing experience to be useful, organized, and easy to verify.

Browse a category, review the product card, then use the Amazon button to confirm the final price, shipping, coupon status, and availability directly on Amazon.

Why the Name Black Lab Deals?

Olive, the original Black Lab Deals quality-control department.

Black Lab Deals is named after Olive, our black lab, who is usually nearby whenever projects are happening around the house.

Whether it's woodworking, home improvement, outdoor projects, tools, or everyday household products, the goal is the same: make it easier to discover useful products and browse current Amazon product information.

Olive may not actually help find product picks, but she definitely supervises the process.
